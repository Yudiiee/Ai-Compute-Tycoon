Privacy policy

AI Compute Tycoon is a single-player offline game. It does not create accounts, collect personal data, use analytics, show ads or share anything with third parties.

Your game progress is saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

The game downloads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time it runs; Google may receive your device's IP address as part of that request. After that the fonts are cached on your device.
